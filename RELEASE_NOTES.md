# v1.0 stable

Play the Mega Man X5 campaign together as X and Zero on Windows PC.

This stable release brings the full campaign refinements from our co-op testing
into one package, from the opening stage through the Maverick and final stages.
It uses the latest tested gameplay build, with refreshed release packaging and
player documentation.

- Independent cameras and life meters keep each hero's view clear.
- Personal pause menus and armor selection give both players their own controls.
- Refined doors, checkpoints, vehicles, capsules and boss transitions keep the
  shared campaign moving.
- Stage backgrounds, environmental effects and combat visuals have received
  extensive attention across the campaign.
- Native memory-card saves preserve progress, while configurable co-op options
  let you choose how to play together.
- Local split-screen and online Host/Join play are included.

Co-op, Alia hint suppression, Adaptive custom rendering and input recording are
enabled for fresh installs. Existing settings remain yours when updating.

Extract the Windows ZIP, run `MegaManX5Recomp.exe`, select your own USA
SLUS-01334 disc image and assign each player's controls. OpenBIOS is included.
See `START_HERE.txt` and the co-op guide for setup.

Keep your native memory cards when updating. Save states are build-specific;
use the game's SAVE and LOAD for durable campaign progress. Online players
should use the same release and compatible mod settings.
