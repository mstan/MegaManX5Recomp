Mega Man X5 @VERSION@ - Stable

1. Extract the package and launch MegaManX5Recomp.exe.
2. Select your own Mega Man X5 USA SLUS-01334 disc image (.cue + .bin preferred).
3. Assign separate controls for Player 1 and Player 2, then press PLAY.
4. Choose Player 1's hero and form, then Player 2's remaining hero and form.

Co-op uses OpenGL and starts with side-by-side views and Either-player doors.
Custom Renderer fits the window using Adaptive aspect; each split pane is 4:3.
Alia hint suppression and Gameplay Input Recording start enabled. Input Display
is optional and starts off; the recording choice applies offline and online. Change
these options in Mods. Your saved settings are retained when updating.

Internal Resolution starts at 4x; larger choices depend on your GPU and memory.
OpenBIOS is included. No game disc, retail BIOS or personal saves are included.

Keep saves/ and your settings when replacing a build. Use native SAVE and LOAD
for durable campaign progress; save states are specific to their build.
Online peers need the same release and compatible mod settings.

See README.md, docs/COOP_GUIDE.md and RELEASE_NOTES.md for more information.
