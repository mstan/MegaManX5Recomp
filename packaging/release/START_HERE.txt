Mega Man X5 @VERSION@

Launch MegaManX5Recomp.exe and select your own USA SLUS-01334 disc image.
OpenBIOS is included. Use OpenGL; keep a normal window and normal audio.

X + Zero Co-op is enabled with local side-by-side cameras and Either-player
doors. Keep Zero remains off. Choose each player's separate controller in the
launcher; the release retains the delivered input.ini/keybinds.ini mappings.
Alia hint suppression, custom rendering and diagnostics remain enabled.

Internal Resolution starts at 4x. Choose 1x to 32x in Mods; hardware and GPU
memory can clamp higher choices. Your saved choices are retained.

This source preserves the v0.2.8 co-op fixes: 23/25 owner reports have bounded
passes; A02/M06 and full-route/LAN limits remain as documented in RELEASE_NOTES.md
and docs/COOP_X5_027_FOLLOWUPS.md. No fresh game-wide qualification is implied.
Trusted overlays remain interpreted; native resident loading/frame waits remain.

Native memory cards live in saves/. Back them up before moving existing cards
with the game closed. No personal cards, settings or QA states are included.
Old snapshots are build-specific and need not work in this build.
